Support

Need help with PassLane? Start with the questions below. If they don't answer it, get in touch.

Contact us

Open an issue at github.com/kingerders-studio/PassLane/issues. Please include your iPhone model, iOS version, the App version (Settings › About) and, for a level problem, the pack and level number. We usually reply within a few days.

How to play

What is the goal?

Get the ball from your player into the goal by passing between your teammates. A pass must travel along a clean lane: a defender in the way, or a pass into a defender's zone, loses the ball. Score in as few passes as possible — reaching par earns three stars.

What is offside?

In packs with offside, a teammate who is closer to the goal line than the last defender when the pass is made is offside, and that pass is not allowed. The offside line is shown on the pitch.

I'm stuck on a level.

Use Undo or Restart, and look for the lane the defenders leave open. You can also use a solution: every player gets free solutions, and you earn one back for every 10 new levels you solve (up to 3). Unlimited solutions are available as a purchase.

Purchases

What can I buy?

PassLane is free to play with the first packs included. Optional one-time purchases: PassLane Pro (all Pro packs and premium kit colours and patterns) and Pro + Solutions (Pro plus unlimited solutions). Pro owners can add a Solutions Upgrade. There are no ads and no subscriptions.

How do I restore my purchases?

Open the App, tap the gear (or your club name) on the home screen to open Settings, then tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple ID you used to buy.

How do I request a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Your club & settings

How do I change my club name, crest or kit?

Tap the gear or your club name on the home screen. Settings also has Sound, Haptics and Appearance (Auto, Light, Dark).

Will I lose my progress if I delete the App?

Progress is stored on your device. Deleting the App removes it; purchases can always be restored with Restore Purchases.
